# Plant watering rota

The three plants in the reading corner, and whose turn it is.

## The plants

- **Ficus** (by the window) — every Monday, a full watering can.
- **Monstera** (next to the armchair) — every other Monday, half a can.
- **Spider plant** (on the shelf above the radiator) — whenever the soil is dry to a
  fingertip, which in winter is about once a fortnight.

## The rota

| Month | Who |
| --- | --- |
| January–March | Priya |
| April–June | Tom |
| July–September | Nadia |
| October–December | Oskar |

Swap with whoever is next on the list if you are away; there is no need to tell
anyone else. The watering can lives under the sink in the kitchen, and the plant food
(one capful per can, in spring only) is on the windowsill beside it.

If a plant looks unhappy, put a note on the whiteboard rather than experimenting.
